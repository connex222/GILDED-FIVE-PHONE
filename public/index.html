Show it on your TV

Your TV needs Chromecast or Google TV built in (or a Chromecast stick), or an Apple TV for iPhone. The TV and your device must be on the same Wi-Fi.

From a laptop (best)

  1. Open this game in Google Chrome.
  2. Click the three dots ⋮ at the top right of Chrome, then Cast…
  3. Under Sources, choose Cast tab, then pick your TV.
  4. Press the TV button in the game. Sound and the announcer play through the TV.

From an Android phone

  1. Swipe down twice from the top of the screen.
  2. Tap Screen Cast, Smart View or Cast (the name depends on your phone), then pick your TV.
  3. Turn your phone sideways and press TV in the game.

From an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Control Centre (swipe down from the top-right corner).
  2. Tap Screen Mirroring and choose your Apple TV.
  3. Turn the device sideways and press TV in the game.

Want everyone on their own phone? Switch on Play with phones on the setup screen (needs the Gilded Five server).

Tip: with a laptop, players can use the keyboard — Space rolls, 1–5 hold dice, arrow keys pick a box, Enter scores, H shows a hint.

How to play

  • Each turn you get up to 3 rolls. Tap a die to hold it so it isn't re-rolled.
  • After any roll, tap a gold number in your column to score it. That box is then used up.
  • If a box would score 0, you must tap it twice — that's called scratching.
  • Everyone fills every box. Highest total wins.
  • Stuck? Press Hint and the game shows the strongest move and why.

Top section

Aces to Sixes: add up only the dice showing that number. Reach 63 or more in this section for a +35 bonus.

Bottom section

  • 3 of a Kind / 4 of a Kind — at least that many matching dice. Scores the total of all five dice.
  • Full House — three of one number and two of another. 25 points.
  • Small Straight — four in a row (e.g. 2-3-4-5). 30 points.
  • Large Straight — five in a row. 40 points.
  • Gilded Five — all five dice the same. 50 points.
  • Chance — anything. Scores the total of all five dice.

Extra Gilded Fives (Joker rule)

If you already scored 50 for a Gilded Five and roll another, you get +100. You must then use the matching top box (e.g. five 4s → Fours) if it's open. If that's taken, any bottom box scores full points. If those are all taken, you must scratch a top box.

Triple Gilded

Every player gets three score columns, worth ×1, ×2 and ×3. You choose which column to use each turn, so the game lasts 39 rounds. Bonuses count per column and are multiplied too.

House rule cards

With this switched on, a gold card is dealt at the start of each round and changes one rule for everyone: Lucky Sevens (1s count as 7), Double Down (Chance doubles), Hot Hand (4 rolls), Tight Table (2 rolls), Full House Party (Full House 40), Straight Shooter (straights +10), Underdog (lowest score that round gets +15) and Safety Net (a scratch still earns 5). Points earned under a card stay on the scorecard. Bonus points go in the Extras row.

Learner and Junior seats

Pick these instead of Person to give someone a helping hand. The best box to score in glows after every roll. A lucky roll is an extra roll they can use once their normal rolls run out (Learner 1, Junior 2). Juniors also start with 25 points. In Triple Gilded the lucky rolls and head start are three times bigger, to match the longer game.

Chips, side bets and the jackpot

Everyone starts with 10 chips. Chips are separate from your score. While someone else rolls, a side bet appears on your phone, such as "Will Dad score more than 20 points?". Stake 1 to 3 chips on Yes or No. Bets close when the second roll starts (or at the first roll for a Gilded Five bet). Winners get their stake back multiplied by the odds shown. Lost chips go into the jackpot, which also grows by one chip every round. Roll a Gilded Five to win the lot. Whoever ends with the most chips is the High Roller.

Power moves

On your turn, after rolling, you can spend chips: Re-roll one die without using a roll (2 chips), Nudge a die up or down by one (3 chips), or Freeze another player so they get one roll fewer next turn (4 chips).

Teams of two

Partners share one scorecard and take turns to roll: one partner plays this turn, the other plays the next. Teams are made from neighbours in the player list (1 + 2, 3 + 4 and so on). Press Shuffle teams to mix them up. A team can include a computer player. Lucky rolls and head starts from Learner and Junior partners are pooled, and the team shares one chip stack.

Replays and shake to roll

Every Gilded Five is shown again in slow motion. On phones, tap Shake at the top and you can roll by shaking your phone. Phones only allow this on a secure (https) address, so it works on the online version but not over home Wi-Fi.

Awards

At the end everyone gets an award, from Gilded Touch and Comeback Kid to Clean Sheet and Hint Hunter. They are read out from last place up to the winner.

Computer players

Set any seat to Easy, Medium or Hard bot. Easy plays on instinct, Medium thinks one roll ahead, Hard works out the odds of every hold.
